# OCP Group: IoT Real-Time Power Quality Monitoring

## 📌 Project Overview
Designed and simulated an Industry 4.0 IoT monitoring system to audit and optimize energy quality within the Phosphoric Acid Plant (PAP) at OCP Group (JFC3/KOFERT). 

## ⚙️ Technical Approach & Methodology
* **IoT Architecture Deployment:** Modeled a smart sensor network for the continuous acquisition of critical electrical data from heavy chemical processing equipment.
* **Energy Quality Auditing:** Monitored real-time energy parameters including harmonics, phase shift, and power factor specific to the PAP environment.
* **Efficiency Optimization:** Simulated corrective scenarios to secure electrical distribution, minimize energy losses, and enhance the overall energy yield of the installation.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* IoT Sensor Networks & Real-Time Monitoring
* Energy Auditing (Harmonics, Power Factor)
* Industry 4.0 Methodologies
* Node-RED & ETAP (Simulation context)

*Note: Due to confidentiality, proprietary OCP data is not shared. This repository highlights the engineering concepts and simulated architectures developed during the internship.*
